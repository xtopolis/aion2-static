Recipes exist as **Elyos/Asmodian pairs that are not identical** — the two sides usually
differ in name, materials and output, so pick your faction rather than expecting a
mirror. Many recipes have a **proc chance** to produce an upgraded item instead of the
normal one. Click any row for its materials and where each comes from.

The Global data lists no kinah or Abyss Point cost on any recipe, so the Kinah column reads
TBD throughout. Each grade-41 slot follows one ladder: a direct craft at the True/Star tier,
transfers up from there, and a Splendent upgrade paid in same-tier draconic materials.
