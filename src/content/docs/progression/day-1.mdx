**Permanent power first, banked resources second, gear last.** Skill levels, Daevanion boards, stigmas and accessories survive every gear
reset; almost nothing you wear before level 45 does.

<Aside type="caution" title="Save your Odyle">
Do not spend odyle while leveling — not on dungeon end chests, not on weapon rolls. The
main story hands you a class weapon that obsoletes anything you gamble for earlier.
Bank it all.
</Aside>

| Sealed dungeons (`?` markers) and regional quests when they're on the way — <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/daevanion-crystal.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Daevanion Crystals</span> are permanent | Manastones, soul binding, stigma leveling and genus rolls on pre-endgame gear |

## At 45: sweep before you queue

One-time solo map content pays permanent power and costs no odyle. Full list: [what to farm](/progression/content/).

1. **Sealed dungeons + strongholds** — <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/daevanion-crystal.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Daevanion Crystals</span>, <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/enhance-stone.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> enhance stones</span>, belt progression.
2. **Regional quests** — regional missions + map exploration pay ~200 Daevanion Crystals per character.
3. <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/monolith/trace-reshanta.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Reshanta feathers**</span> — the ~200–220 lying around take about two hours and level the PvP Monolith.
4. **Mainland rift windows** (~every 3 h, ~1 h on the enemy continent) — their sealed dungeons pay ~500 <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/abyss-points.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> AP</span> each plus Daevanion points and belt scrolls.

Anything that works in both PvE and PvP — Daevanion nodes, stigma shards, skill levels,
accessories — is a safe sink from hour one. It never gets replaced.

## Breaking 1,000 CP

The cheapest CP comes right after the main story:

1. **Fill every slot** — Pilgrim accessories from Lv48–50 regional quests, plus the
   quest Rune Box (a +0 rune is still 40 iLvl).
2. **Growth gear to +4** — kinah cost inflects after +4. Spares to +7, quest Uniques
   and the guaranteed piece to +10.
3. <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/stones/lesser-manastone.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Lesser manastones only**</span>, never reroll — and skip theostones: their damage
   is irrelevant at low tiers.
4. **Exploration stages: quick grabs first** — boss/chest-grab stages take seconds and
   push CP across the 1,000 / 1,200 / 1,600 gates; leave the timed-puzzle and RNG stages
   for when you're overgeared.
5. At 1,200: **Transcendence 1-1** — the arcana card alone is ~+20 iLvl.

## 1,000 → 2,000

Two different things give CP, and mixing them up is what makes this stretch feel slow:

- **Clearing content** gives a modest bump — the gear that drops, once you equip it. Worth
  roughly **+50 CP per expedition clear** in the benchmark run.
- **Spending what it dropped** gives the spikes. Arcana card levels, manastones engraved
  into gear, enhance attempts: **+134, +127 and +153** in single sittings.

So run content to stock the bank, then sit down and convert. Don't do one without the other
— a clear you never cash in is worth a fraction of what it could be.

| Step | Run | Then spend |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 → 1,400 | Lv 45+ quest sweep — Spacetime Rift chains, story epilogue | Daevanion crystals into the board (+79 and +57 in back-to-back sittings) |
| 1,400 → 1,600 | <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/odyle-energy.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Draupnir</span> (CP 900), Nightmare, Transcendence stages | Bank the arcana cards and manastones — don't engrave yet |
| 1,600 | **Vakron Sky Island** the moment you qualify (weekly, 2–5 players) | Equip the drops |
| 1,600 → 2,000+ | — | Everything at once: arcana levels, manastones onto the new gear, then an enhance pass |

<Aside type="caution" title="The one route mistake">
75 minutes in the Abyss moved the benchmark's CP by **7**. The Abyss pays Abyss Points, not
gear score — go once the gear curve flattens, not during the push.
</Aside>
