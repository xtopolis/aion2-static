## Alts

**Alts help main?** answers one question: *does farming this on an alt directly improve my
main?* Kinah, manastones, enhance stones and tradeable crystals all move across through
account storage, and account-wide collections like the closet count no matter who filled
them. Abyss Points, skill points and anything spent on a character's own boards stay put.

Three lockouts are worth running on ~3 alts:

- **Ascension Trials** — manastones and enhance stones, which move to your main.
- **Corridors** and **Battlegrounds** — both per character, both worth running on alts. See
  [The Loop (PVP)](/progression/the-loop-pvp/).
