## Abyss Points

The weekly cap **accumulates rather than resets** — if it is 1M, week two is 2M and week
five is 5M, so missing weeks costs you nothing. Combat AP is what it counts, and **supply
requests are exempt**. Once capped, mobs stop paying AP and ranking, but player kills still
pay ranking and Spacetime Rift still pays a reduced amount.

Points buy PvP gear, stigma shards, and PvP manastones that craft up and sell into whale
demand. The **1M daily kinah cap is open-world only**, not inside the Abyss — which still
does not make Abyss farming lucrative next to dungeon kinah.

## Alts

Two PvP lockouts are counted **per character**, so alts genuinely add to the account:

- **Battlefield** — equalized, so a fresh 45 performs. Each alt's weekly reward is a choice
  between an Azphel board crystal and pure kinah.
- **Corridors** — the AP stays on the alt, but it buys cheap abyss gear to break down for
  skins, and the closet is account-wide.

Abyss Points themselves do not transfer. See [The Loop (PVE)](/progression/the-loop-pve/#alts)
for what does.
