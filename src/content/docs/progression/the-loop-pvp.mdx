PvP is optional. The timed modes are large enough that numbers matter more than gear, and
Abyss commands pay stigma shards, so a PvE player still wants a foot in here. PvE lives on
[The Loop (PVE)](/progression/the-loop-pve/).

extra hours are the top-ten ranked, who need kills to hold rank.
</Aside>

## Abyss Points

<Aside type="caution" title="The cap accumulates, it does not reset">
If the weekly cap is 1M, week two is 2M, and week five is 5M. Miss three weeks and you come
back to three weeks of headroom, not a lost quarter. **You never lose banked cap**, so there
is no urgency to hit it every week.
</Aside>

Combat AP is what the cap counts. **Supply requests are exempt** &mdash; fill the combat cap
first, then extend without limit through turn-ins. Once capped, mobs stop paying AP and
ranking, but **player kills still pay ranking**, and Spacetime Rift still pays a reduced
amount.

Points mostly buy PvP gear and <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/stigma-shard.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> stigma shards</span>. They also buy PvP manastones, which craft to higher
tiers and sell into whale demand.

<Aside type="caution" title="Abyss is not a kinah printer">
The **1M daily kinah cap applies to the open world only**, not inside the Abyss. That does
not make Abyss farming lucrative &mdash; dungeon kinah is what funds progression. Abyss
farming is filler for when everything else is done.
</Aside>

## Alts

Two PvP lockouts are counted **per character**, so alts genuinely add to the account:

- **Battlefield** &mdash; equalized, so a fresh 45 performs. Each alt's weekly reward is a
  choice between an Azphel board crystal and pure kinah.
- **Corridors** &mdash; the AP stays on the alt, but it buys cheap abyss gear to break down
  for skins, and the closet is account-wide.

Abyss Points themselves do not transfer. See [The Loop (PVE)](/progression/the-loop-pve/#alts)
for what does.
