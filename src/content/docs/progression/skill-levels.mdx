- **Active skills open a specialization option at level 8, 12 and 16.** You get one
  specialization point at each of those levels, so you end up choosing **3 of the 5** options
  a skill offers. Passives have no specializations, and stigmas run their own ladder off
  stigma shards.
- **Skill points and the Daevanion boards take a skill to 14.** <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/wisdom-stone.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Wisdom Stones**</span> cover
  1–10 and the first four boards add **+4**. Every level past that, on actives and passives
  alike, comes from **soul binds and arcana cards**.

## Arcana

Each arcana card draws from a fixed slice of your skill list:

| Card | Pool | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/chalice-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Chalice**</span> | 22 | all active and passives |
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/scales-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Scales**</span> | 22 | all active and passives |
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/parchment-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Parchment**</span> | 6 | half your actives |
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/compass-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Compass**</span> | 6 | the other half |
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/bell-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Bell**</span> | 5 | half your passives |
| <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/arcana/mirror-of-punishment.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Mirror**</span> | 5 | the other half |
| **Hourglass** · **Key** | — | no skill lines at all |

A <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> card holds **4 skill lines, each enhanceable to +4** —
up to 4 actives on a Parchment or Compass, 4 passives on a Bell or Mirror, or any mix of the
two on a Chalice or Scales — and transmuting one lets you name those skills specifically
rather than rolling for them.

## Soul bind

How many lines a piece rolls is set by its grade:

| Grade | <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/slots/weapon.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Weapon · Guard · Ring</span> | <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/slots/chest.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Earrings · Necklace · Armor</span> |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-common">Common</span> | 1 | 1 |
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | 2 | 2 |
| <span class="g-legend">Legend</span> | 3 | 3 |
| <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> | 3–5 | 4–5 |
| <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> | 4–6 | 5–6 |

<span class="g-gold">Unique</span> and <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> pieces add a bonus
**Soul Fuse** line once every soul bind on them sits at 100%.

You have roughly a **2%** chance of landing the skill you want on a line: **80%** of the
choices are stats and **20%** are skills.

|---|---|
