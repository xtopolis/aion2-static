<Aside type="tip" title="Abyss Point caps">
Combat Abyss Points are capped per week. **Supply requests are not** — fill the combat cap
first, then extend without limit through turn-ins. Once capped, mobs stop paying AP and
ranking, but **player kills still pay ranking**.
</Aside>

## Alts

The **Alt farm** column is about what reaches your main through account storage — kinah,
manastones, enhance stones and tradeable crystals all move; Abyss Points, skill points and
anything spent on a character's own boards do not.

Three lockouts are worth running on ~3 alts, about an hour a week without battlegrounds:
**Corridors**, **Ascension Trials** and **Battlegrounds**. Corridors and Trials feed the
main's manastone and enhance-stone income; battlegrounds are gear-equalized, so a fresh 45
earns at full rate. Each alt's battleground slot is a weekly choice between an Azphel
board crystal and pure kinah.

<span class="c-warn">Stigma shards are character-bound</span> — an alt's shards only ever
help that alt.
