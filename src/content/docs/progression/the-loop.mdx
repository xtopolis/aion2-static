income is gone — these are the ones worth building a session around.

<Aside type="tip" title="The weekly AP shape">
Combat Abyss Points are capped per week. **Supply requests are not** — fill the combat cap
first, then extend without limit through turn-ins.
</Aside>

## Abyss bosses

Three sets, three cadences.

| Boss set | Spawns |
|---|---|
| **Enraged** | Around **xx:20**, following Artifact Wars (twice weekly) |
| **Kaira** | Random chance at **every :00** |
| **Nahmas** | Set days and times — reported as **Thursday and Sunday** on TW |

## Alts

Three lockouts are worth running on ~3 alts, about an hour a week total: **Corridors** and
**Ascension Trials** feed the main's manastone and enhance-stone income through account
storage. Battlegrounds only if you're chasing the Azphel board.

<span class="c-warn">Trial shards are character-bound</span> and can't be moved to your main.
