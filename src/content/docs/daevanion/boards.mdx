Every node costs points, from **1 for a Common node up to 4 for a Unique** one, and you
buy outward from the free centre. Each class has five boards. Filling Nezekan, Zikel or
Vaizel costs **134 points**, Triniel **168** and the level-45 Azphel board **232**, so all
five together come to **802**. The first four carry skill levels and differ per class;
Azphel is pure PvP stats, identical for every class, and is paid for with Daevanion
Crystal: Azphel rather than the regular Daevanion Crystal.
