Every node costs points, from **1 for a Common node up to 4 for a Unique** one, and you
buy outward from the free centre. Filling one of the first four boards costs **210
points**; each of the level-45 boards costs **232**, so all eight together come to
**1,768**. Boards 1–4 are the ones that carry skill levels and differ per class; boards
5–8 are pure stats and identical for every class.
