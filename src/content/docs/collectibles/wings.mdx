Every wing carries two stat blocks. The **Equip Effect** applies only while the wing
is worn, and you wear one at a time. The **Owned Effect** applies permanently once the
wing is in your collection and stacks across every wing you own, the same way title
collection stats do. The table below lists the Owned Effect and Flight Power. Stat wings
enchant to **+10**, which raises Flight Power and, on a few Rare and Legendary wings,
adds one more owned line; **8 of the 33 wings** (every Special-grade skin) grant
nothing and are purely cosmetic.

<Aside type="note" title="Getting a wing at Global launch">
Conquest dungeons drop a **Sealed** wing (Krao Cave, Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon, Vakron
Sky Island, Fire Temple, Ferocious Horn Den). The sealed wing is tradable and morphs
into the usable wing, which is bound; day-one reports put the morph at 10 Radiant Odyle
and say a sealed wing can be morphed into enhancement stones instead.
</Aside>
