Killing an open-world monster drops its **soul**; enough souls unlock that monster as a
pet-mount and further souls level it. Every pet you own adds permanent stats — shared
across your characters on the server — whether it is equipped or not, and the same
collecting feeds each family's [Genus Insight](#genus-insight) rolls.

## What the collection gives

208 pets across five families. Every pet in a family grants the identical stat ladder, so
what matters is only *how many* of each family you own and their level. Souls per pet run
5 / 25 / 75, so **5 unlocks it, 30 gets it to level 2 and 105 to level 3**, and level 3 is
the cap.

Soul sources beyond field kills: open-world cubes drop 5-soul bundles, the black
merchant's pet tab sells 5 souls of a rotating pet hourly, and the Odylium Repository
daily dungeon pays up to 80 pet chests a run — the single biggest faucet, and worth
running on alts since the progress is shared. It is in the Global data but was not open
during the launch scale test, so expect open-world kills to carry the first weeks.

## Genus Insight

Each family has an Analysis track of **9 slots**; slot *N* opens at analysis level *N*.
Souls earned for an already-maxed pet convert into that family's **soul crystals**,
which fuel Analyze rolls — one kill, one crystal. Two other faucets: **duty quests** roll
crystals among their random rewards, and hidden cubes pay soul crystals in bulk (1,000
per keyed cube in both day-one reports). One roll costs **5 soul crystals and 1,000
kinah** and rerolls **every unlocked slot at once** from that slot's option pool at its
grade — **lock** the lines you want to keep, knowing locks cost crystals and kinah that
climb by slot (10 and 2,000 on slot 1, 495 and 99,000 on slot 8). Grades run
<span class="g-common">Common</span> · <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> ·
<span class="g-legend">Legendary</span> · <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> ·
<span class="g-heroic">Mythic</span>, with better pools and higher values at the top.

**Your analysis level decides which grades can roll.** Early levels turn up mostly
<span class="g-common">Common</span> and <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> lines;
<span class="g-gold">Unique</span> becomes likely as you climb, and
<span class="g-heroic">Mythic</span> only opens at the top of the track. That is the
argument for pushing a track to the top before you start locking anything — every lock
you place early is a line you rolled from a worse pool.

Slots share option tables in fixed groups. Values below are the Mythic pool; slots 4 and 7
are the only ones that differ by family:

| Slots | Pool | Pick |
|---|---|---|
| **1, 5** | Flat attacks: Attack Bonus 8 – 16, Max Attack 10 – 20, Critical / Front / Back / PvE / Boss Attack 10 – 20, Penetration 100 – 200, plus HP and MP fillers | **Attack Bonus** — it sits inside the percentage multipliers, and Max Attack behind it |
| **2, 8** | Defenses only: HP increase 1.2 – 2.4%, Defense 80 – 160, Critical Damage / Front / Back / PvE / Boss Defense 100 – 200, crit-resist lines, plus HP and MP | **HP increase**; there is no offensive line to take instead |
| **3, 6, 9** | Accuracy Bonus 20 – 40, Critical Hit 15 – 30, Block 25 – 50, Evasion Bonus 15 – 30, Front / Back Attack Critical Hit 25 – 50, PvE Accuracy 10 – 20, Power Shard Damage Bonus 8 – 16, plus HP and MP | **Critical Hit / Accuracy Bonus** until you reach the [80% caps](/progression/stats/#soft-caps); after that Power Shard Damage Bonus is the only offensive line left |
| **4** | Offensive percentages: PvE Damage Boost 2 – 4%, own-family Damage Boost 2.4 – 4.8%, Endurance / Regeneration Penetration 1.5 – 3%, Status Effect Chance 1.5 – 3%, plus two family lines at 1.5 – 3%: Cogni **Damage Boost** and **Critical Damage Boost**, Fera **Damage Boost** and **Weapon Damage Boost**, Natura Multi-hit Chance and Perfect Chance, Varian Frontal and Back Attack Damage Boost | **PvE Damage Boost** for pure PvE; Damage Boost on Cogni or Fera if you also PvP |
| **7** | Mirror of slot 4 on defense: PvE Damage Tolerance 2 – 4%, own-family Damage Tolerance 2.4 – 4.8%, Endurance, Regeneration, Status Effect Resist 1.5 – 3%, the family pair's tolerances, Defense 80 – 160 | Cap filler; **PvE Damage Tolerance** is the safe pick on every family |

The **Special** track (Black Dragon and friends) is identical except on slots 4 and 7:
slot 4 rolls <span class="g-heroic">**Smite 1.5 – 3%**</span>, Damage Boost, Critical
Damage Boost, Frontal / Back Attack Damage Boost, Endurance Penetration and Status
Effect Chance; slot 7 mirrors it with Damage Tolerance, Critical Damage Tolerance,
Frontal / Back tolerances, Endurance, Smite Resist and Status Effect Resist. That one
Special slot is the **only Smite in Genus Insight**.

Each track has three roll pages — a practical split is one PvP page, one short-term
page that fills your crit and accuracy caps, and one long-term page you slowly perfect.

## What to chase

Verdicts follow the site's [Key Stats](/progression/stats/) tiers.

| Verdict | Line | Where |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | <span class="g-heroic">Smite</span> (S tier) | Special genus slot 4 only, every class |
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | PvE Damage Boost, Damage Boost | Genus slot 4 on the four normal families |
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | Attack Bonus | Genus slots 1, 5 |
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | Critical Hit and Accuracy Bonus | Genus slots 3, 6, 9 until capped — the pet collection's own 82 crit + 130 accuracy count toward the same caps |
| <span class="c-keep">Free</span> | Everything in the collection tables | Just kill trash on your path while levelling; the stats bank account-wide and never expire |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Perfect Chance | D tier — roll over it on Natura slot 4 |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Penetration, Power Shard Damage Bonus, attack variants (Critical / Front / Back / PvE Attack) | Attack variants sit outside the percentage multipliers; Penetration is throttled by its ×0.1 coefficient — see the [attack formula](/progression/stats/#attack-formula) |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Locking defensive rolls | Slots 2, 7 and 8 hold nothing but defense, so take HP increase and PvE Damage Tolerance there and spend your locks elsewhere |
