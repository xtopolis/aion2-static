Killing an open-world monster drops its **soul**; enough souls unlock that monster as a
pet-mount and further souls level it. Every pet you own adds permanent stats — shared
across your characters on the server — whether it is equipped or not, and the same
collecting feeds each family's [Genus Insight](#genus-insight) rolls.

## What the collection gives

292 pets across five families. Every pet in a family grants the identical stat ladder, so
what matters is only *how many* of each family you own and their level. Souls per pet:
15 to reach level 2, then 50 / 100 / 180. The level cap is 5 on TW; **Global launches
capped at level 3**, so the level 4–5 stats below (including every Family Damage Boost)
are not reachable there yet.

Soul sources beyond field kills: open-world cubes drop 5-soul bundles, the black
merchant's pet tab sells 5 souls of a rotating pet hourly, and the pet daily dungeon
pays out roughly 10 souls per chest across dozens of chests — the single biggest
faucet, and worth running on alts since the progress is shared.

## Genus Insight

Each family has an Analysis track of **9 slots**; slot *N* opens at analysis level *N*.
Souls earned for an already-maxed pet convert into that family's **insight crystals**,
which fuel Analyze rolls. One roll rerolls **every unlocked slot at once** from that
slot's option pool at its grade — **lock** the lines you want to keep, knowing each lock
makes later rolls more expensive. Grades run
<span class="g-common">Common</span> · <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> ·
<span class="g-legend">Legendary</span> · <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> ·
<span class="g-heroic">Mythic</span>, with better pools and roughly doubled values at
the top.

Slots share option tables in fixed groups, identical across families:

| Slots | Pool | Pick |
|---|---|---|
| **3, 9** | Damage multipliers: Damage Boost and its Weapon / Critical / Front / Back / PvE / family variants, <span class="g-heroic">Smite</span>, Perfect Chance, plus the generic flat lines | <span class="g-heroic">**Smite — always**</span> (1.2 – 2.4% at Mythic). These are its **only** two slots, it appears at <span class="g-gold">Unique</span>+ grade only, at ~5% per roll — lock any high roll on sight |
| **2, 5, 8** | Flat attacks: Attack Bonus, Max Attack, Critical / Front / Back Attack, Boss Attack, PvE Attack, plus crit / accuracy / HP fillers | **Attack Bonus** (8 – 16 at Mythic) — the only genus attack line that sits inside the percentage multipliers |
| **1, 4, 7** | Defenses: Defense, Block, HP, crit-resist and positional defenses, plus Critical Hit, Accuracy Bonus and Boss Attack | **Critical Hit / Accuracy Bonus** until you reach the [80% caps](/progression/stats/#soft-caps), then **Boss Attack** |
| **6** | Tolerances: Damage Tolerance and its variants, Endurance, Regeneration | Cap filler; a high **PvE Damage Tolerance** roll is acceptable |

The **Special** track breaks the pattern: slots 1 through 8 share one all-offense pool
(Attack Bonus, Max Attack, Penetration, Power Shard Damage…), so it builds offensively
in every slot, and Smite rolls there at every grade instead of Unique+.

Each track has three roll pages — a practical split is one PvP page, one short-term
page that fills your crit and accuracy caps, and one long-term page you slowly perfect.

## What to chase

Verdicts follow the site's [Key Stats](/progression/stats/) tiers.

| Verdict | Line | Where |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | <span class="g-heroic">Smite</span> (S tier) | Genus slots 3 and 9, every family, every class |
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | Attack Bonus | Genus slots 2, 5, 8 |
| <span class="c-keep">Chase</span> | Critical Hit and Accuracy Bonus | Genus slots 1, 4, 7 until capped — the pet collection's own 112 crit + 184 accuracy count toward the same caps |
| <span class="c-keep">Free</span> | Everything in the collection tables | Just kill trash on your path while levelling; the stats bank account-wide and never expire |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Perfect Chance | D tier — roll over it even on slots 3/9 |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Penetration, Power Shard Damage, attack variants (Critical / Front / Back / PvE Attack) | All sit outside the percentage multipliers — see the [attack formula](/progression/stats/#attack-formula) |
| <span class="c-warn">Skip</span> | Defensive and tolerance rolls beyond slot 6 | Offense is king in PvE; slot 6 already covers the defensive budget |
