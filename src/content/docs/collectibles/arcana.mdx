Every card carries a **god stat** and rolls **skill levels**, and cards belong to sets that
pay bonuses at 2 and 4 pieces. The god stat is the part you plan around — it is how you
reach [Smite, Combat Speed and Attack Increase](/progression/stats/#where-the-points-come-from)
outside of gear.

## Which card carries which god stat

The seven sets split into two families that hand out completely different stats from the
same card. A <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> card is worth **20 points** of its stat
(Libra 10, Key 5, Hourglass 50), and every enchant level adds one more.

**Indomitable Dedication is the hybrid** — Time on its Chalice and Wisdom on its Mirror,
so it reaches into both families.

<Aside type="tip" title="The three cards that matter">
**Chalice → Time**, **Bell → Destruction**, **Mirror → Wisdom.** Those are the top three
god stats for PvE, and no two of them come from the same family — which is what forces a
split set build.
</Aside>

## Set bonuses

| Set | 2 pieces | 4 pieces |
|---|---|---|
| **Punishing Overture** | +5% Boss Damage Tolerance | +60 PvE Attack, **+10% PvE Damage Boost** above 70% HP |
| **Protected Soul** | +5% Restoration | **+5% Weapon Damage Boost**, 10,000-damage shield below 30% HP |
| **Indomitable Dedication** | +5% Weapon Damage Tolerance | +5% Critical Damage Tolerance, +50% PvP Damage Tolerance when crowd-controlled |
| **Pure Blood** | +500 PvE Defense | +5% Critical Damage Boost, +1,000 Defense below 70% HP |
| **Frenzy** | +50 PvE Attack | +5% Boss Damage Boost, +10% Boss Damage Tolerance below 70% HP |
| **Primal Vigor** | +60 PvE Attack above 70% HP | +150 PvE Attack above 70% HP |
| **Magic Armor** | Restores 1,500 MP below 20% MP | +1,000 PvE Defense above 50% MP |

## The KR meta build

Every one of the nine classes converges on the same combination — see
[PVE](/meta/pve/#arcana) for the numbers.

**4 Protected Soul · 2 Indomitable Dedication · 4 Punishing Overture**

It is not arbitrary. Punishing Overture supplies the Bell and Mirror
(<span class="c-keep">Destruction</span> and <span class="c-keep">Wisdom</span>), Protected
Soul supplies the Chalice (<span class="c-keep">Time</span>), and you still land both
4-piece bonuses: **+10% PvE Damage Boost** and **+5% Weapon Damage Boost**.

## Enchanting and crafting

Cards enchant to **+5**, and each level adds a point of the card's god stat plus a level to
one of its skill lines. Crafting through Transmute lets you pick the lines instead of
rolling for them — **five Mysterious Crystals** make a
<span class="g-gold">Unique</span> card, and the Chalice is the one to build first.

<Aside type="caution" title="Global will not launch with all of this">
The KR meta above assumes the full card and set pool. Global is expected to open with
roughly five card types and two sets, so treat the combination as a destination rather than
a day-one plan.
</Aside>
