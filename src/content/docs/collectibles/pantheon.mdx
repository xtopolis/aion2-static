Everything you place in the Pantheon pays **god stats**, which are worth
[0.2% per point](/progression/stats/#where-the-points-come-from) — twice the rate of base
attributes. Nothing here is equipped in the gear sense; you collect it and place it, and it
pays whether or not you ever look at it again.

The best colossus carries as much as **eight** of the best artworks, or twenty average ones.
If you are choosing what to chase, that is the whole answer.

## The colossi worth chasing

<span class="g-gold">Destruction</span>, <span class="g-gold">Time</span> and
<span class="g-gold">Wisdom</span> are the three god stats worth planning around — Attack
Increase, Combat Speed and Smite. Three colossi pay nothing but those.

**Ascended Atheron is the pick of them** — Guardian Lord Nahma ties it on raw points, but
spends a third of them on Justice, which is close to dead weight in PvE. The same applies to
Kromede's Desire and Zikel's Apparition: two-thirds as good as they look.

<Aside type="tip" title="Artworks are still worth it early">
They are small individually, but they are cheap and they add up. Three artworks at
3 Wisdom each is 9 points — about **1.8% Smite** — which is a real return for what
Shugo Festival points cost.
</Aside>

## Where the points are

Totals across everything in the Pantheon. For how these rank against each other, see
[Key Stats](/progression/stats/#where-the-points-come-from) — the practical bracelet
ordering there is Destruction > Wisdom > Illusion > Time.

| God stat | What it gives | Points available |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-gold">Wisdom</span> | Smite | 418 |
| <span class="g-gold">Time</span> | Combat Speed | 353 |
| <span class="g-gold">Destruction</span> | Attack Increase | 283 |
| <span class="g-legend">Illusion</span> | Cooldown Reduction | 323 |
| <span class="g-legend">Death</span> | Critical Hit | 380 |
| <span class="g-legend">Freedom</span> | Accuracy, Evasion | 336 |
| <span class="g-rare">Space · Destiny · Life · Justice</span> | Move Speed, MP, HP, Defense | 1,153 combined |

Wisdom being the most plentiful *and* the highest-value stat is the happy accident here —
the Pantheon is the cheapest Smite on offer.
