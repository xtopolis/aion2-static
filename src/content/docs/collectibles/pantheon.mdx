Everything you place in the Pantheon pays **god stats**, which are worth
[0.2% per point](/progression/stats/#where-the-points-come-from) — twice the rate of base
attributes. Nothing here is equipped in the gear sense; you collect it and place it, and it
pays whether or not you ever look at it again.

artworks. If you are choosing what to chase, that is the whole answer.

**Ascended Atheron is the single best item in the Pantheon** — 150 points, all three of them
S-tier. The Justice on the last three is close to dead weight in PvE, so they are two-thirds
as good as they look.

<Aside type="tip" title="Artworks are still worth it early">
They are small individually, but they are cheap and they add up. Three artworks at
3 Wisdom each is 9 points — about **1.8% Smite** — which is a real return for what
Shugo Festival points cost.
</Aside>

## Where the points are

Totals across everything in the Pantheon, ordered by what our
[Key Stats](/progression/stats/) page rates them.

| God stat | What it gives | Points available |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="c-keep">Wisdom</span> | Smite | 418 |
| <span class="c-keep">Time</span> | Combat Speed | 353 |
| <span class="c-keep">Destruction</span> | Attack Increase | 283 |
| Illusion | Cooldown Reduction | 323 |
| Death | Critical Hit | 380 |
| Freedom | Accuracy, Evasion | 336 |
| Space · Destiny · Life · Justice | Move Speed, MP, HP, Defense | 1,153 combined |

Wisdom being the most plentiful *and* the highest-value stat is the happy accident here —
the Pantheon is the cheapest Smite on offer.
