Abyss gear carries a PvE penalty that scales with how many pieces you wear — which makes
the **first** piece free and every piece after it a trade. Numbers below are the
<span class="g-gold">Guardian Decanus</span> tier, the entry set.

## The set penalty

Every abyss set runs the same ladder. The PvE loss is **cumulative** — a full set is −20%.

The **PvP weapon is not part of the set** — it never counts toward the penalty. Tiers count
separately too, so Centurion pieces and Decanus pieces build two independent totals.

The Guard costs double the PvP damage of an accessory but **four times the price of a
ring**. It is the least efficient piece in the shop on both stats.

### So what does the Guard actually buy

The penalty-free slot. Spend one Guard's worth of AP three ways:

| Buy | Pieces | PvP damage boost | Attack | PvE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Guard alone** | 1 | 10% | 85 | <span class="c-keep">no penalty</span> |
| 2 Rings <span class="mx-note">63,200 AP</span> | 2 | 10% stat + 5% set = **15%** | 68 | <span class="c-warn">−5%</span> |
| 2 Rings + Earrings <span class="mx-note">105,300 AP</span> | 3 | 15% stat + 5% set = **20%** | 119 | <span class="c-warn">−5%</span> |

Two rings beat the Guard on PvP damage for **half the AP**. Three accessories double it and
still come in cheaper. The Guard's entire case is the −5% PvE damage. It's worth considering to share with your PvE build without incurring a penalty.

<Aside type="tip" title="Pick by which game you're actually playing">
**Doing both:** Guard first. It's the only piece that costs you nothing in PvE, it upgrades
an offhand slot where your PvE alternatives sit at iLvl 50–55, and it rolls Active skill
lines.

**Committed to PvP:** skip it. Two rings and earrings give twice the PvP damage for less
AP, and you were going to eat the −5% eventually anyway.
</Aside>

### Accessory order

Once you've accepted the penalty, all three give identical PvP damage boost — so buy on
price and what else they carry:

1. **Ring** — cheapest per point of PvP damage, **two slots**, and rolls **Active** skill
   lines. Skill levels 16 and 20 come only from arcana cards and ring soulbinds.
2. **Earrings** — next cheapest, Passive lines.
3. **Necklace** — the most attack and defense of any accessory, and the best attack per AP,
   but one slot and Passive lines. Buy it for raw stats, not for skills.

Armor comes last in every case: it buys damage *tolerance*, not damage.

## Enhance order

Take **all your PvE gear to +10 first**, then start on PvP gear. PvP pieces reach +5
cheaply; put the Guard at **+10** and leave the rest at **+5** until your PvE set clears
the content you need it to.

Past that point, spend on <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/stigma-shard.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **stigma shards**</span>
rather than abyss armor.

## Weapons

| Class type | Take |
|---|---|
| **Melee** | An **extend** weapon — it doubles auto-attack range and is considered mandatory. Every **Fire Temple** weapon is extend; Ludra and sanctuary weapons also qualify |
| **Ranged** | Just use your PvE weapon. The PvE damage bonus outweighs the PvP lines and keeps you clearing PvE content |

## Stat lines for PvP

The build is **aggressive, not defensive** — you win by landing hits, not by living longer.

- **Every defensive line → Accuracy.** If the target has block or evasion and you can't
  beat it, you never connect.
- **Offensive lines: roughly 20% Attack Bonus, the rest Accuracy.**
- **Genus lines 3 and 9 → Smite**, same as [PvE](/collectibles/pets/).
- Not every class wants evasion or block — check which one your passives buff.

Running a hybrid? Use the [PvE soulbind layout](/gear-enhancement/soul-binding/); it holds
up for most cases.

## Feathers are the cheapest PvP damage you'll get

PvP feathers raise the PvP monolith, which pays Daevanion points for the PvP board.
Clearing every season-1 feather unlocks all the PvP damage boost tiles: **+7.5% PvP
damage**. Stack the 2-piece set on top and you're at **+12.5%** before any gear quality
matters — decisive at launch, when most players have no defensive stats at all.
