Abyss gear carries a PvE penalty that scales with how many pieces you wear — which makes
the **first** piece free and every piece after it a trade. Numbers below are the
<span class="g-gold">Guardian Decanus</span> tier, the entry set.

## The set penalty

<span class="g-gold">Guardian Decanus</span> is the entry set, and its bonus ladder pays
**nothing in PvP** — only damage against abyss monsters, and a **cumulative −5% PvE** at
every threshold.

So at Decanus **every piece past the first is a straight −5% PvE for no set reward** — all
your PvP damage comes from the items' own stats. The abyss-damage and PvE columns are the
same on every tier, but the PvP column is not: Centurion and Tribunus add +5% at some
thresholds, High Commander +10%. Stacking pieces starts paying for itself later, not here.

The **PvP weapon is not part of the set** — it never counts toward the penalty. Tiers count
separately too, so Centurion pieces and Decanus pieces build two independent totals.

### So what does the Guard actually buy

The penalty-free slot. Set a Guard's price against what the same AP buys in accessories:

| Buy | Pieces | PvP damage boost | Attack | PvE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Guard alone** <span class="mx-note">126,200 AP</span> | 1 | **10%** | 85 | <span class="c-keep">no penalty</span> |
| 2 Rings <span class="mx-note">63,200 AP</span> | 2 | **10%** | 68 | <span class="c-warn">−5%</span> |
| 2 Rings + Earrings <span class="mx-note">105,300 AP</span> | 3 | **15%** | 119 | <span class="c-warn">−5%</span> |
| 2 Rings + 2 Earrings <span class="mx-note">147,400 AP</span> | 4 | **20%** | 170 | <span class="c-warn">−5%</span> |

Two rings match the Guard's 10% for **half the AP** — but cost you 5% PvE to do it. The
Guard's entire case is avoiding that: it's the one PvP piece worth sharing with your PvE
build at no cost.

<Aside type="tip" title="Pick by which game you're actually playing">
**Doing both:** Guard first. It's the only piece that costs you nothing in PvE, it upgrades
an offhand slot where your PvE alternatives sit at iLvl 50–55, and it rolls Active skill
lines.

**Committed to PvP:** skip it. Two rings and earrings give 15% for less AP than the Guard's
10%, and you were going to eat the −5% eventually anyway.
</Aside>

### Accessory order

All three give identical PvP damage boost, and **ring and earring slots are doubled while
the necklace is a single** — so the first four buys are two rings and two earrings. Order
them on price and what else they carry:

1. **Ring** — cheapest per point of PvP damage, and rolls **Active** skill lines. Skill
   levels **15+** come only from arcana lines and ring or necklace soulbinds, so these
   carry value beyond the PvP stat.
2. **Earrings** — half again the attack of a ring per slot (51 vs 34) for a third more AP.
   Passive lines. Take these first if you want stats now over skill levels.
3. **Necklace** — the most attack and defense of any single accessory and the best attack
   per AP, but it fills one slot and rolls Passive lines.

Armor comes last in every case: it buys damage *tolerance*, not damage.

## Enhance order

Take **all your PvE gear to [+11](/gear-enhancement/enhancing/) first**, then start on PvP
gear. PvP pieces reach +5 cheaply; put the Guard at **+10** and leave the rest at **+5**
until your PvE set clears the content you need it to.

Past that point, spend on <span class="icon-label"><img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/stigma-shard.webp" alt="" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **stigma shards**</span>
rather than abyss armor.

| Class type | Take |
|---|---|
| **Melee** | An **extend** weapon — it doubles auto-attack range and is considered mandatory. Every **Fire Temple** weapon is extend; Ludra and sanctuary weapons also qualify |
| **Ranged** | Just use your PvE weapon. The PvE damage bonus outweighs the PvP lines and keeps you clearing PvE content |

## Stat lines for PvP

The build is **aggressive, not defensive** — you win by landing hits, not by living longer.

- **Every defensive line → Accuracy.** If the target has block or evasion and you can't
  beat it, you never connect.
- **Offensive lines: roughly 20% Attack Bonus, the rest Accuracy.**
- **Genus lines 3 and 9 → <span class="g-heroic">Smite</span>**, same as [PvE](/collectibles/pets/).
- Not every class wants evasion or block — check which one your passives buff.

For a hybrid build, use the [PvE soulbind layout](/gear-enhancement/soul-binding/); it
holds up for most cases.

## Feathers are the cheapest PvP damage you'll get

PvP feathers raise the PvP monolith, which pays Daevanion points for the PvP board.
The Azphel board carries **four PvP Damage Boost tiles at 2.5% each** — clearing every
season-1 feather is **+10% PvP damage** for no gear at all. That is more than any two abyss
pieces will give you at Decanus, and it costs no PvE penalty.
