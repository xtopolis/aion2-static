title: Theostones (Weapon only)
description: Every Aion 2 theostone, what it procs, how often, and where to get one.
---

Theostones engrave into a weapon's single **Theostone slot** and give it an on-hit proc. You
pick the stone, so nothing is rolled.

Their contribution to damage is **negligible**, under 1% of your total even at Unique grade.
Each one also puts a colored glow on the weapon, so most players pick whichever proc looks
best rather than chasing numbers. Engraved theostones are <span class="c-warn">lost when you
transfer gear</span>.

## Types

One slot per weapon, and only on **Legend grade and above**. The offhand Guard counts as a
weapon, so a shield user runs two procs at once.

Seventeen effects, each sold in three grades. The grade changes only the size of the number,
never the proc chance, and the same effect never stacks with itself.

| Grade | What it changes |
|---|---|
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | weakest value |
| <span class="g-legend">Legend</span> | middle value |
| <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> | strongest value |

Within a group the proc chance and the damage always cancel out: every <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> single
target stone averages 150 damage per swing, and every <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> area stone 75 per
target. Picking Wind over Water buys frequent small hits instead of rare large ones, never
more damage, so choose on how it feels and how it looks.

## How to obtain

| Grade | How to get it |
|---|---|
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/rare-theostone-chest.webp" alt="Rare Theostone Chest" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Abyss Shop: Rare Theostone Chest, <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/abyss-points.webp" alt="Abyss Points" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 35,000 AP<br />Transcendence trial box<br />Sanctuary raid, reward pool 4<br />Hidden open-world cubes |
| <span class="g-legend">Legend</span> | Transcendence trial box, later stages<br />Sanctuary raid, reward pool 4<br />Combine from Rare |
| <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> | Combine from Legend |

Sanctuary's theostone pool runs roughly 89% Rare to 11% Legend. **Combine** merges lower grades
upward for a *chance* at the next grade, the way the Closet combines skins, rather than at a
fixed rate.

No Unique theostone appears anywhere in the captured drop tables, which cover 36 dungeon and
tier combinations, so combining looks like the only route to one. Those captures do not cover
Abyss sieges, corridors or rifts, so treat this as unconfirmed rather than settled.
