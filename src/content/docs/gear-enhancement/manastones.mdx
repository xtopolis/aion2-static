<img src="/icons/gear/superior-manastone.webp" alt="" width="24" style="vertical-align:middle" /> **Manastones** go in weapons and armor, <img src="/icons/gear/superior-soulstone.webp" alt="" width="24" style="vertical-align:middle" /> **Soulstones** in accessories — same roll tables, same costs. Each stone rerolls **all** of the item's rows at once: every row gets a random stat, then a random quality. Rolls are <span class="c-no">lost on transfer</span>.

<Aside type="tip" title="Recommended">
Early on, use Lesser stones only and don't chase rerolls — gear gets replaced long before the stones pay off. Save Superiors for endgame pieces.
</Aside>

## Types

| Stone | Stat pool | Basic | Superior | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesser | 1 of 9 | 70% | 30% | — |
| Intermediate | 1 of 9 | 50% | 35% | 15% |
| Superior | 1 of 14 | 50% | 35% | 15% |
| Lesser Abyssal | 1 of 16 | 50% | 35% | 15% |
| Intermediate Abyssal | 1 of 16 | 50% | 35% | 15% |
| Superior Abyssal | 1 of 22 | 50% | 35% | 15% |

Abyssal stones add PvP stats to the pool (slightly diluting the PvE ones); the tier sets the values and the shot at Ultimate quality.

## How to obtain

| Stone | Source |
|---|---|
| Lesser | Drops, vendors |
| Intermediate | Craft (Alchemy) |
| Superior | Craft proc (20% off Intermediate)<br/>Ordeal Shop (2,000 for 5 /wk) |
| Lesser Abyssal | Abyss Shop (1,000 AP mana / 2,000 AP soul) |
| Intermediate Abyssal | Craft (Alchemy) |
| Superior Abyssal | Craft proc (20% off Intermediate Abyssal)<br/>Ordeal Shop, soulstone only (6,000 for 5 /wk) |
