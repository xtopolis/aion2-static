<span class="c-mana">**Manastones**</span> go in weapons and armor,
<span class="c-soul">**soulstones**</span> in accessories. They are otherwise the same item,
with identical roll tables and costs. Each row rolls a random stat, then a random quality
for that stat. Engraved stats are <span class="c-warn">lost when you transfer gear</span>.

## Types

**Abyssal stones add PvP stats and slightly reduce the chance of PvE ones**; everything else
about them is the same. The tier decides how much each stat gives, and how often you get the
top result.

## Available stats

**You get one stat per row, and every row is rolled on its own.** A four-row weapon produces
four results from a single engrave, and the same stat can land more than once. The `1 of 9`
above means each row has a chance at one of nine stats (Core stats, below). Higher tier stones
or Abyssal variations add more options to the pool.

## How it works, example

Each row is decided in two steps:

1. **Which stat** it lands on. The two *Chance to roll* columns are the odds per stat, and
   each column adds up to 100%.
2. **How good the roll is**: Basic, Superior or Ultimate. Those are the three numbers in
   every value cell, in that order.

Here is an example using a **Superior Manastone**.

**Step 1: the game picks a stat.** It reads down that stone's *Chance to roll* column (1 of 14
possible stats), where Attack sits at 10%:

| Stat | Chance |
|---|--:|
| Attack | **10%** |
| Critical Hit | 10% |
| Damage Boost | 0.5% |
| Defense | 10% |
| HP | 13.75% |
| …and 9 more | |

Say it lands on **Attack**.

**Step 2: the game picks a quality.** A separate roll, 50/35/15 on this stone. Attack's
three numbers in the table above are `3 / 5 / 10`, so those are the three values it can
possibly be worth:

| Quality | Odds | You get | Row color |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| Basic | 50% | Attack **+3** | <span class="g-rare">green</span> |
| Superior | 35% | Attack **+5** | <span class="g-legend">blue</span> |
| Ultimate | 15% | Attack **+10** | <span class="g-gold">gold</span> |

Say it lands on **Basic**.

**Result:** that row now reads **Attack +3** and shows as a green row. The odds of
landing exactly that come to 10% × 50% = **5%**.

| Stone | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Lesser** | Drop or vendor only |
| **Intermediate** | Craft (Alchemy) |
| **Superior** | Craft (Alchemy)<br />20% proc off the Intermediate craft<br />Ordeal Shop (2,000 for 5 a week) |
| **Lesser Abyssal** | Abyss Shop: <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/abyss-points.webp" alt="Abyss Points" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 1,000 AP manastone<br />Abyss Shop: <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/abyss-points.webp" alt="Abyss Points" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 2,000 AP soulstone |
| **Intermediate Abyssal** | Craft (Alchemy) |
| **Superior Abyssal** | 20% proc off the Intermediate Abyssal craft, no recipe of its own<br />Ordeal Shop (6,000 for 5 a week), soulstone only |

Early on, use **Lesser stones only** and do not chase rerolls; gear gets replaced long before
the stones pay off.
