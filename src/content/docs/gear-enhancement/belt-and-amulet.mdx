The belt and PvE/PvP amulets come from the **MSQ**. These are **permanent additions** you'll upgrade over time. Once an item reaches **+10**, you can **morph** it to the next grade.

<Aside type="tip" title="Unique is the top">
<span class="g-unique">Unique</span> is the highest grade of belt or amulet in the Global data; there is no Heroic step to save for.
</Aside>

## Which amulet

Same item, one stat swapped. **PvE → Revelation. PvP → Fierce Battle. Endgame → both.**
