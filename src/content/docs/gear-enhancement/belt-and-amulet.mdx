The belt and PvE/PvP amulets come from the **MSQ**. These are **permanent additions** you'll upgrade over time. Once an item reaches **+10**, you can **morph** it to the next grade.

<Aside type="tip" title="Stop at Unique">
Recommend to get to <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> and then focus on other gear upgrades before going for <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span>.
</Aside>
