Soul binding is available only on <span class="g-gold">Unique</span> and <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> gear, but on every slot from weapon to
brooch: 2,462 of the 5,713 pieces in the client data. Legend and below get none of it. Each piece
holds **3 to 6 soul bind slots**, 4 being the common case, and what lands in them is drawn at
random from that slot's pool when you acquire the item. Binding then raises those stats toward
their ceiling, so it changes how high they sit, never which ones you have.

Unlike manastones and theostones, every one of those base stats
<span class="c-keep">carries over when you transfer gear</span>, at the value you bound it to.
The single exception is the extra stat unlocked with Philosopher's Stones, which
<span class="c-warn">does not transfer</span>. That is why soul
binding is worth doing on gear you already plan to replace.

## Types

Three operations share the Soul Binding tab.

| Operation | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Bind** | Raises the value of your existing stats. Always succeeds, by random amount | <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/kinah.webp" alt="Kinah" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 30,000 + <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/soul-codex.webp" alt="Soul Codex" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Soul Codexe(s) |
| **Sync** | Rerolls one stat into a different one | <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/sync-stone-fragment.webp" alt="Sync Stone Fragment" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Sync Stone, or same grade gear |
| **Reset** | Shuffles every stat on the piece at once | <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/soul-codex-reset.webp" alt="Soul Codex: Reset" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> Soul Codex: Reset |

Every slot on a piece has to be maxed before the extra slot unlocks, which is exactly what puts
that piece's Soul Binding Rate at 100%. From there, **2 Philosopher's Stones and <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/kinah.webp" alt="Kinah" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 1,000,000
kinah** add one more slot. There is no RNG on the unlock itself: it always succeeds, and it
works on any soul bindable piece.

That extra slot is the one part of soul binding a transfer does not preserve.

| On a transfer | |
|---|---|
| The **3 to 6 base stats**, at their bound values | <span class="c-keep">carry over</span> |
| The Philosopher's Stone extra stat | <span class="c-warn">does not transfer</span> |

So the stones are spent again on every new piece, and the stat they unlock is rolled again from
scratch. Everything you bound into the base slots follows the item untouched.

## Available stats

Each slot draws once. **One draw in five lands on a class skill** instead of a stat, giving
it +1 level. Weapons, guards and **rings** draw from the 12 actives your class uses with
that weapon; armor, earrings and necklaces draw from its 10 passives. Weapons are class
locked, so a dagger only ever rolls assassin skills.

The other four draws in five land on a stat, and the tables below are the odds within that
stat draw, so each one totals 100%. Which stats are on offer depends on where the piece is
worn: every weapon shares one pool, every accessory shares another, and armor shares a common
core plus a few stats unique to each slot.

Three of those are worth planning around: **Chest** is the only armor source of Damage Boost,
**Shoulder** the only source of Critical Damage Boost, and **Gloves** the only armor source of
Combat Speed. **Smite** rolls on the helmet and cape only.

Read the percentages as a rarity ranking. The scarcest rolls are the strongest ones, so a 2%
Damage Boost on a weapon is the prize and an 8% HP is filler.

## How it works, example

Each Bind raises one stat toward its ceiling. Two things decide the outcome:

1. **Which stats you have.** Fixed when the gear was acquired, changeable only by Sync or
   Reset.
2. **How high each one binds.** Every Bind is a guaranteed increase, but higher values get
   progressively less likely.

Here is an example using a Unique dagger whose Critical Attack sits at 48 with a ceiling of
51.

**Step 1: you Bind.** It costs <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/currency/kinah.webp" alt="Kinah" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> 30,000 plus Soul Codex, and the increase is guaranteed.
The codex count is not fixed at one: it climbs with each bind on that piece, so the number
the screen asks for grows as the stat approaches its ceiling.

**Step 2: the game picks a value.** Each step up the ladder is rarer than the last:

| Critical Attack | Chance of binding to it |
|---|--:|
| 49 | 42.33% |
| 50 | 32.60% |
| 51 | 25.07% |

**Result:** the stat moves up, its yellow bar advances, and the blue Soul Binding Rate
follows. Longer ladders taper much harder. On the same dagger HP runs from 226 to 265, and
the top of that range binds at **0.65%**.

## How to obtain

| Material | Used for | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/soul-codex.webp" alt="Soul Codex" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Soul Codex** | Every Bind | Dungeon drops, Transcendence, Sanctuary, Nightmare shop, Shugo Festival |
| <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/soul-codex-reset.webp" alt="Soul Codex: Reset" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Soul Codex: Reset** | Reset | Nightmare shop, quest rewards |
| <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/materials/sync-stone-fragment.webp" alt="Sync Stone Fragment" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /> **Sync Stone** (<span class="g-gold">Unique</span>, <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span>) | Sync | Sanctuary, or morph Sync Stone Fragments from dungeons. Any spare Unique piece also morphs into one |
| **Philosopher's Stone: Revelation** | The extra slot, on most pieces | Conquest dungeons |
| **Philosopher's Stone: Power** | The extra slot, on the rest | Conquest dungeons |

Both Philosopher's Stones are spent two at a time, and roughly four pieces in five take
Revelation. Which one a piece needs is stamped on the item and cannot be predicted from its
grade, tier or item level: two Unique boots at the same item level can want different stones,
so check the piece before farming.

A Sync Stone below the gear's grade applies only **10%** of the time, so never sync <span class="g-gold">Unique</span>
stones into <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> gear.
