<span class="g-unique">Unique</span> and <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> gear only, every slot from weapon to brooch. Each piece has **3–6 stat lines** (usually 4), rolled when you acquire it. **Bind** raises their values toward a ceiling — it changes how high they sit, never which stats you have. Bound base stats <span class="c-yes">transfer</span>; the Philosopher's Stone extra stat <span class="c-no">does not</span>.

<Aside type="tip" title="Recommended">
Soul bind even interim gear — the investment follows the item through transfers. Hold Philosopher's Stones for pieces you'll keep 3+ weeks.
</Aside>

## Operations

| Operation | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="/icons/gear/soul-codex.webp" alt="" width="22" style="vertical-align:middle" /> Bind | Raises a stat's value (always succeeds, amount is RNG) | 30,000 kinah + Soul Codex (count climbs per bind) |
| <img src="/icons/gear/sync-stone-fragment-unique.webp" alt="" width="22" style="vertical-align:middle" /> Sync | Rerolls one stat into a different one | Sync Stone or same-grade gear |
| Reset | Shuffles every stat at once | Soul Codex: Reset |
| <img src="/icons/gear/sealed-philosophers-stone.webp" alt="" width="22" style="vertical-align:middle" /> Extra slot | Adds one more stat line, always succeeds | 2× Philosopher's Stone + 1,000,000 kinah — requires all lines maxed (100% Soul Binding Rate) |

Bind amounts taper: each step toward the ceiling is rarer than the last (a 3-point Critical Attack ladder rolls ~42/33/25%; a 40-point HP ladder's top binds at 0.65%).

## Roll pools

Each line draws once: **1 in 5 lands a class skill (+1 level)** — actives on weapons, passives on armor/accessories — the other 4 in 5 land a stat from the slot's pool. **Scarcer = stronger**: 2% rolls are the prizes, 8–10% rolls are filler.

## How to obtain

| Material | For | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Philosopher's Stone: Revelation / Power | Extra slot (×2 per unlock) | Conquest dungeons |
| Soul Codex | Bind | Dungeon drops, Transcendence, Sanctuary, Nightmare shop, Shugo Festival |
| Soul Codex: Reset | Reset | Nightmare shop, quests |
| Sync Stone (Unique / Heroic) | Sync | Sanctuary, morph Sync Stone Fragments or spare Unique gear |

Which Philosopher's Stone a piece wants (Revelation ~4 in 5, Power the rest) is stamped on the item — check before farming. A Sync Stone below the gear's grade only applies 10% of the time; never sync Unique stones into <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> gear.
