<img src="/icons/gear/theostone.webp" alt="" width="24" style="vertical-align:middle" /> One slot per weapon and guard (<span class="g-legend">Legend</span> grade up), giving an on-hit proc and a colored weapon glow. You pick the stone — nothing is rolled. <span class="c-no">Lost on transfer</span>.

<Aside type="tip" title="Pick for looks">
Total damage contribution is under 1% even at Unique grade — choose the proc and glow you like. Within each damage group, proc chance × damage is identical, so Wind vs Fire is feel, not power.
</Aside>

Three grades per effect — <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> / Epic / <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> — scaling only the value, never the proc chance. Identical effects don't stack.

## How to obtain

| Grade | Source |
|---|---|
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | Abyss Shop chest (35,000 AP)<br/>Hidden open-world cubes<br/>Sanctuary raid (pool 4, ~89% of its theostones)<br/>Transcendence trial box |
| Epic | Combine from Rare<br/>Sanctuary raid (~11%)<br/>Transcendence trial box, later stages |
| <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | Combine from Epic (no drops in any captured table — presumed combine-only) |

Combine merges lower grades upward for a chance at the next, like the Closet's skin combine.
