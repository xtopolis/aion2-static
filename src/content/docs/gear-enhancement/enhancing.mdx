Weapons, guards, armor and accessories can be **enhanced** up to +15 (cap depends on grade). After that, the **Exceed** track upgrades the piece up to +5 more times. Enhance level <span class="c-yes">transfers</span> to a new item; exceed level <span class="c-no">does not</span>.

<Aside type="tip" title="Recommended upgrade order">
Slot priority: **Weapon > Guard** > accessories > armor — weapon and guard carry the biggest damage gains per stone.

1. Everything to **+11**
2. Weapon + accessories to exceed **+2**
3. Weapon to exceed **+5**
4. Accessories the rest of the way
5. Armor to exceed **+2**
</Aside>

## Enhance

| Grade | Enhance cap | Exceed |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-common">Common</span> | +5 | — |
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | +5 | — |
| <span class="g-legend">Legend</span> | +10 | — |
| <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | +15 | +5 |

Common, Rare and Legend never fail. Unique is guaranteed through +10, then **65% / 50% / 35% / 25% / 20%** for +11 to +15; each failure adds 5% to the next attempt, and no level is lost.

## Exceed

Same kinah and stone cost for every slot, and the same odds on every Unique piece. Each failure adds the pity % to your next attempt. How far a piece can go depends on its item level: **86 and above** reach +4, **70 to 78** stop at +2, and anything lower cannot exceed at all.

| Step | <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | Pity | Kinah | Stones | Weapon gain (cumulative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 | 50% | +4% | 900,000 | 9 | Attack +12 to 17<br/>Damage Boost +1% |
| +2 | 33% | +3% | 1,400,000 | 12 | Attack +23 to 34<br/>Damage Boost +2% |
| +3 | 25% | +2% | 2,000,000 | 15 | Attack +35 to 50<br/>Damage Boost +3% |
| +4 | 20% | +1% | 3,000,000 | 20 | Attack +46 to 67<br/>Damage Boost +4% |
| +5 | ? | ? | ? | ? | Attack +58 to 84<br/>Damage Boost +5% |

Every step eats Amplify Stones (Unique); the attack figure scales with the weapon. Armor steps add Damage Tolerance 0.5%, HP increase 0.5% and flat Defense and HP; accessories add Damage Boost 0.5% and flat Attack and Defense. The +5 step's stats are in the client but its cost and odds are not.

Average cost, exceed +1 → +4, per piece:

| Grade | Attempts | Kinah | Stones |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | ~12 | ~25M | ~188 |

## What survives a transfer

Transfer always succeeds and moves your investment onto a new item.

| <span class="c-yes">Transfers</span> | <span class="c-no">Lost</span> |
|---|---|
| Enhance level | Exceed level |
| Skill levels rolled on gear | Manastones / Soulstones |
| Soul Binding (base stats) | Soul Binding (Philosopher's Stone extra stat) |
| | Theostones |
| | Potential stats |

Soul Binding is safe to invest in interim gear; manastones and theostones are not.

## Potential

A fourth track on every Unique piece, crafted or dropped, and in the Global data it is a **PvP** track. Each step adds 0.5%, to 2% at step 4. Steps 1 to 3 always succeed; step 4's cost is not in the client. Does not transfer. No weapon carries a PvE Damage Boost line any more, so crafted and dungeon gear of the same item level no longer differ here.

| Slot | Stat | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapon, earring, necklace, ring | PvP Damage Boost | 2 stones | 3 stones | 4 stones |
| Armor, bracelet | PvP Damage Tolerance | 2 stones | 3 stones | 4 stones |

Every step also costs **100,000 Abyss Points**. The stone is **Potential Stone: Abyss (Unique)**, which drops bound from Abyss mobs.
