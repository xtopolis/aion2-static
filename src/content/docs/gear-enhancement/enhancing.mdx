Weapons, guards, armor and accessories can be **enhanced** up to +20 (cap depends on grade). After that, the **Exceed** track upgrades the piece up to +5 more times. Enhance level <span class="c-yes">transfers</span> to a new item; exceed level <span class="c-no">does not</span>.

<Aside type="tip" title="Recommended upgrade order">
1. Everything to **+11**
2. Weapon + accessories to exceed **+2**
3. Weapon to exceed **+5**
4. Accessories the rest of the way
5. Armor to exceed **+2**
</Aside>

## Enhance

| Grade | Enhance cap | Exceed |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-rare">Rare</span> | +10 | — |
| <span class="g-legend">Legend</span> | +15 | — |
| <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | +15 | +5 |
| <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> | +20 | +5 |

Enhancing is guaranteed through +10, with pity above that (community-reported; the client ships no odds).

## Exceed

Same kinah and stone cost for every slot; only grade changes the odds. Each failure adds the pity % to your next attempt.

| Step | <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> | Pity | Kinah | Stones | Weapon gain (cumulative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 | 50% | 25% | +4% | 900,000 | 9 | Attack +30<br/>Damage Ratio +1% |
| +2 | 33% | 16.5% | +3% | 1,400,000 | 12 | Attack +60<br/>Damage Ratio +2% |
| +3 | 25% | 12.5% | +2% | 2,000,000 | 15 | Attack +90<br/>Damage Ratio +3% |
| +4 | 20% | 10% | +1% | 3,000,000 | 20 | Attack +120<br/>Damage Ratio +4% |
| +5 | ? | ? | ? | ? | ? | Attack +150<br/>Damage Ratio +5% |

Stones match the item's grade: Unique pieces eat Unique stones, Heroic eat Heroic. The +5 step's stats are in the client but its cost and odds are not.

Average cost, exceed +1 → +4, per piece:

| Grade | Attempts | Kinah | Stones |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="g-unique">Unique</span> | ~12 | ~25M | ~188 |
| <span class="g-heroic">Heroic</span> | ~19 | ~40M | ~294 |

## What survives a transfer

Transfer always succeeds and moves your investment onto a new item.

| <span class="c-yes">Transfers</span> | <span class="c-no">Lost</span> |
|---|---|
| Enhance level | Exceed level |
| Skill levels rolled on gear | Manastones / Soulstones |
| Soul Binding (base stats) | Soul Binding (Philosopher's Stone extra stat) |
| | Theostones |
| | Potential stats |

Soul Binding is safe to invest in interim gear; manastones and theostones are not.

## Potential

A hidden quality on every piece — the reason crafted gear beats dungeon gear of the same grade. Crafted items ship at max. Dungeon items start low and **can be raised** with Potential Stones (morphed from spare gear). Does not transfer.

| Slot | Crafted | Dungeon |
|---|---|---|
| Accessory | 2.5% | 1.2% |
| Armor | 5% Damage Tolerance | 1.2% Damage Tolerance |
| Weapon | 5% Damage Boost | 2.4% Damage Boost |
